# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in VibeMind a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy, consent, and project security boundaries;
- focusing disagreements on ideas and evidence, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private
information without permission, or other conduct that would reasonably make a
person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement actions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options
on the [@Flissel GitHub profile](https://github.com/Flissel). Do not use a public
issue for a sensitive conduct report. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project in public spaces.
